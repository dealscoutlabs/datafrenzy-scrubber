9. Limits and attestation

Safe Harbor also requires that the seller has no actual knowledge that the remaining information could identify a person, alone or combined with other information. This tool flags common risks: unique roles, rare conditions together with places, small-town and newsworthy references, and relationship references. It cannot know everything the seller knows. Automated detection is not perfect, and its measured recall on synthetic test data is not a guarantee for real data. Pseudonym tokens are consistent within this export, and the key that links them stays on the seller's computer and is never exported.

This report records how the data was processed. It is not a legal opinion. For an Expert Determination (45 CFR 164.514(b)(1)), give the expert this report, the manifest and the tool's documentation.
